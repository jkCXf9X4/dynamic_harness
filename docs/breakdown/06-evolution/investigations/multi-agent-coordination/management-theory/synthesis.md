# Synthesis: What the Theory Says

Section 5 of the management-theory survey. Index: `management-theory`. One
line per theory: key idea to leadership act to agent design principle.

- **Information theory (Shannon)** — relays add noise & latency to keep hops
  minimal to direct peer edges; parent never retransmits.
- **Transaction costs (Coase/Williamson)** — coordination has a price to
  internalize cheap links, avoid expensive relay to `introduce` = 1 turn; relay =
  O(n) turns.
- **Bounded rationality (Simon)** — the hub's attention is the ceiling to don't
  make the hub do everything to parent touches boundaries only.
- **Micromanagement research / Hamel** — control tax destroys throughput to align,
  don't approve to no content relaying, ever.
- **Conway's law (inverse)** — structure follows desired comms to build the right
  structure to team = common parent, forged via `introduce`.
- **Context over control (Netflix)** — give the why, not the how to coach, step in
  only at risk to injection = context; parent exits the channel.
- **Highly aligned, loosely coupled (Netflix)** — alignment enables autonomy to
  align on direction, stay out of exchanges to roles + intro notes = alignment.
- **Hackman's five conditions** — team works if structure is right to set
  membership, direction, norms, then coach minimally to real team = parent group;
  norms = policy; coach = connect/disconnect/escalate only.
- **Team of Teams (McChrystal)** — shared consciousness to empowered execution to
  build the shared picture, then decentralize to intro = shared consciousness;
  peers self-synchronize.
- **Transactive memory (Wegner)** — knowing who knows what is the asset to be the
  knowledge broker, don't reshuffle to parent = directory + router; prefer
  introduce over re-delegate.
- **Project Aristotle** — psychological safety predicts performance to make
  speaking up safe to ask/escalate/decline are safe, cheap, non-punitive.
- **Edmondson** — failure framing determines learning to frame work as learning;
  invite questions to failures carry notes forward (informational, not blame).
- **Servant leadership (Greenleaf)** — leader removes obstacles to serve, connect,
  resource to parent as enabler, not producer.
- **Structural holes / weak ties (Burt, Granovetter)** — brokers + novel links
  create value to bridge non-obvious connections to introduce across scopes.
- **Deliberate dissent / cognitive diversity** — diverse challenge beats uniform
  consensus to build dissent in to occasional contrarian/verifier introductions.
- **Rituals over control (agile)** — cadence replaces permission to pattern the
  rhythm to `[child settled]` cadence + settle-then-yield rule.
