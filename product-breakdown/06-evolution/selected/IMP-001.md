## Theme
Firm up the communication-topology comparison by running more than one
replicate per cell.

## Evidence

  "Cells: 3 · Task modes: interdependent · Replicates: 1"; per-cell summary
  shows n=1 for `off` (timed_out, 0 turns, 900s), `shared` (completed, correct,
  40 turns, 389.3s, 799,955 tokens), `topics_parent` (completed, correct,

- `product-breakdown/04-verification/communication-structures/metrics-cells.json`

- `product-breakdown/04-verification/communication-structures/FINDINGS.md` —
  "Caveat: n=1 per cell, pricing unconfigured (cost $0), and the run was
  interrupted once by my own kill — treat the ratios, not the absolutes, as
  signal." Also: "Hypothesis to test next: the parent-declared channel set
  (`findings`) plus per-topic watermarks pushed agents into more verification /
  topic-policing back-and-forth, or repeated `channel_read` polling; a bigger
  replicate budget + per-tool turn breakdown (from the traces) would separate
  'LLM verbosity' from 'harness overhead'."

## Current Pain Or Risk
The single most important collaboration decision (AD-001: reject parent-mediated
relaying; AD-003: workspace-primary) rests on a **single replicate per cell**.
The `shared`-vs-`topics_parent` ratio (~6× churn) is a hypothesis, not a
measurement; the `off` deadlock is confirmed twice (FINDINGS reproduction +
probe log) but the other cells' numbers could shift with more runs. A future
run could re-search this design space (violating the repo's ground rule) or
adopt a topology on noise.

## Proposed Improvement
Run the P4 real-LLM battery with **multiple replicates per cell** (e.g. n=3–5)
on the interdependent collab task, plus a per-tool turn breakdown from the
traces to separate LLM verbosity from harness overhead. Report mean + spread
per cell in RESULTS.md / metrics-cells.json; keep the watchdog and the
mock-first determinism bed unchanged.

## Risk And Blast Radius
Low. Cost: real-LLM tokens per run (the `topics_parent` cell alone consumed
~5.2M tokens in one run — budget accordingly; pricing was unconfigured at
$0.00). Blast radius: confined to the verification layer and the AD-001/AD-003
evidence base; no runtime code changes. Risk: the churn hypothesis may be
confirmed as harness overhead, which would reopen the AD-002/AD-004
parent-authorized-channel design — that is the point of measuring.

## Task Contract Seed
- **Objective:** Produce a statistically meaningful per-cell comparison of the
  four communication topologies on the interdependent collab task.
- **Scope:** ≥3 replicates per cell (off, siblings, shared, topics_parent) via
  `python -m dynamic_harness.benchmark.run_comms`; per-tool turn breakdown from
  traces; updated RESULTS.md + metrics-cells.json with mean/spread; a short
  findings note on the `topics_parent` churn hypothesis.
- **Acceptance:** each cell has ≥3 completed-or-timed_out runs recorded in
  metrics-cells.json; RESULTS.md reports mean ± spread per metric; the churn
  hypothesis is confirmed or refuted with trace evidence; no runtime code
  changes outside the benchmark bed.

## Out Of Scope

  out of scope for the bed).
- Adding new topology cells beyond the four.
- Pricing/cost configuration.
