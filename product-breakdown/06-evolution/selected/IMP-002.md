# IMP-002: Workspace Mechanics + Round/Cadence Semantics for Collaboration Groups

## Lifecycle Stage
Proposed

## Status
Open

## Layer
Architecture

## Theme
Define and implement the shared-workspace mechanics and round/cadence semantics that AD-003 (workspace-primary) leaves open.

## Evidence
- `../../02-architecture/multi-agent-coordination/INVESTIGATION.md` §"What this does to B vs C…": residual items the theory does not decide — **Workspace mechanics**: a scoped `ArtifactStore` area needs write-conflict conventions and visibility rules the store does not enforce (C's known gap); **Cadence/round semantics**: "which children are waiting on a sibling's write" (settle-then-yield becomes "fetch newly-settled siblings once per turn").
- Same file, "Investigation next steps": spec the workspace mechanics (scoped `ArtifactStore` area + visibility + append/review conventions) and round/cadence semantics.
- `../../02-architecture/multi-agent-coordination/collaboration-setting/README.md` — the advanced-layer spec whose AC-1..11 depend on these mechanics.
- AD-003 (workspace-primary channel), AD-006 (spine; workspace deferred to the advanced policy layer).

## Current Pain Or Risk
AD-003 is adopted but its substrate does not exist: no scoped team area, no write-conflict conventions (append/review over overwrite), no visibility rules. The "shared workspace" is a concept, not a mechanism; children cannot safely coordinate, and conflicting writes / stale reads (option C's cons) become real. Cadence is undefined, so the asynchronous workspace either starves (no one reads) or thrashes (everyone polls).

## Proposed Improvement
Specify and implement, as a policy layer on the AD-006 spine: (1) a scoped team-workspace area in `ArtifactStore` with visibility rules (member writes scoped to the introduced team, per AD-005) and append/review conventions (blackboard/git practice); (2) round/cadence semantics — "fetch newly-settled siblings' writes once per turn", with a per-round cap.

## Risk And Blast Radius
Medium. Risk: over-engineering (locking/versioning the store does not enforce) or shipping conflicting writes / stale reads. Blast radius: the collaboration feature area (comms layer, agent loop, policies) — the workspace is the primary substrate, so a wrong design affects all patterns (handoff, critique, merge, shared-target). Mitigation: build on the AD-006 spine (links + pointers) first; the workspace is a policy layer, not core.

## Dependencies

- IMP-004 (`introduce`) — the workspace is opened by the founding parent, so introductions and workspace creation are coupled.
