## Current Pain Or Risk
The workspace-primary decision (AD-003) is adopted, but its substrate does not
exist: the `ArtifactStore` has no scoped team area, no write-conflict
conventions (append/review over overwrite), and no visibility rules. Without
them, the "shared workspace" is a concept, not a mechanism — children cannot
safely coordinate on a shared surface, and conflicting writes / stale reads
(option C's known cons) become real. Cadence is undefined: a child has no rule
for when to fetch newly-settled siblings' writes, so the asynchronous workspace
either starves (no one reads) or thrashes (everyone polls).

## Proposed Improvement
Specify and implement, as a policy layer on the AD-006 spine: (1) a scoped
team-workspace area in `ArtifactStore` with visibility rules (who may
read/write what, when — member writes scoped to the team workspace they were
introduced to, per AD-005) and append/review write conventions (blackboard/git
practice: append-only + review over overwrite); (2) round/cadence semantics —
"fetch newly-settled siblings' writes once per turn" as the settle-then-yield
reinterpretation for a shared surface, with a per-round cap.

## Risk And Blast Radius
Medium. Risk: over-engineering the workspace (locking/versioning conventions
the store doesn't enforce today) or, conversely, shipping a workspace that
allows conflicting writes and stale reads. Blast radius: the collaboration
feature area (comms layer, agent loop, policies) — the workspace is the
primary collaboration substrate, so a wrong design affects all collaboration
patterns (handoff, critique, merge, shared-target coordination). Mitigation:
build on the AD-006 spine (links + pointers) first; the workspace is a policy
layer, not core.

## Task Contract Seed
- **Objective:** Make the shared team workspace a working mechanism with
  defined visibility, write conventions, and cadence.
- **Scope:** scoped team-workspace area in `ArtifactStore`; visibility rules
  (member writes scoped to the introduced team); append/review write
  conventions; round/cadence semantics ("fetch newly-settled siblings' writes
  once per turn" + per-round cap); policy objects per the AD-006 spine;
  mock-LLM tests for a handoff and a merge pattern.
- **Acceptance:** a child can read/write the team workspace scoped to its
  introduced team; conflicting writes are prevented or resolved by the
  append/review convention; a child fetches newly-settled siblings' writes
  once per turn with a per-round cap; `pytest` green; the collaboration-setting
  spec's AC-1..11 workspace-related items are executable.
