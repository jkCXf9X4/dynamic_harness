## Current Pain Or Risk
The repo's core promise is "verify before synthesize" and "every sub-agent's
output verified (artifact read, content confirmed)" (VISION success criterion
#1), but verification is only prompt advice: the sole mechanical check is file
*existence*, not content or correctness. A child can report a deliverable that
does not meet the acceptance criteria and the runtime will accept it; the
parent's VERIFY step is as reliable as the model's obedience — and the repo's
own self-healing analysis (self-healing.md) shows the model "rarely complies"
with prompt-level instructions. This is a P0 gap: it breaks the trustable-
orchestration promise.

## Proposed Improvement
Mechanize verification: a `verify` policy that inspects deliverable *content*
against the plan's acceptance criteria at settlement — starting with the
cheapest reliable form (e.g. pointer-target inspection per AD-006: the verify
policy checks that referenced artifacts exist, are non-empty, and match stated
acceptance markers), then graduating to content checks where a verifier can be
mechanical (the benchmark verifiers already prove the pattern). The team
charter (AD-005) becomes the mechanical acceptance target for team output.

## Risk And Blast Radius
Medium. Risk: over-mechanizing (a brittle verifier that rejects good work or
accepts bad work) or under-mechanizing (a verifier that only checks existence,
which is today's gap). Blast radius: the core agent lifecycle — every
delegation's termination is gated by verification, so a wrong verifier affects
all runs. Mitigation: start with the cheapest reliable form (pointer-target
inspection), keep the model's VERIFY as the content-level step, and measure
false-accept/false-reject on the benchmark suite before widening.

## Task Contract Seed
- **Objective:** Acceptance criteria are mechanically checked at settlement —
  verification is a mechanism, not prompt discipline.
- **Scope:** a `verify` policy (per AD-006) inspecting pointer targets at
  settlement; acceptance-criteria recording wired to the check; mechanical
  checks for the benchmark verifiers; G1 marked resolved in gap-analysis when
  the mechanism ships.
- **Acceptance:** a child whose deliverable fails the acceptance check is
  flagged/healed by the mechanism (not by prompt); the check catches a
  deliberately-bad deliverable in a mock-LLM test; the benchmark suite's
  verifiers run through the mechanism; `pytest` green; gap-analysis G1 status
  updated to resolved with evidence.
