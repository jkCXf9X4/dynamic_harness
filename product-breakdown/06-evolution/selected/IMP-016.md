# IMP-016: Refactor product-breakdown nodes to the AD-009 budget

## Lifecycle Stage
Completed

## Status
Implemented (2026-09-23)

## Layer
Architecture

## Theme
Bring all `product-breakdown/` nodes within the AD-009 budget and empty the allow-list.

## Evidence
`python3 product-breakdown/tools/check_node_size.py` reports 52 grandfathered
nodes — the largest `02-architecture/multi-agent-coordination/INVESTIGATION.md`
at 903 lines — plus the 8 ADRs at 93–121 lines. The exact set is
`product-breakdown/tools/node_size_allowlist.txt`.

## Current Pain Or Risk
The definition state is hard to retrieve from: long investigation nodes mix
current state with superseded reasoning, and decision records exceed the
decision-record budget. The node model (AD-009) is adopted but unenforced until
the allow-list empties.

## Proposed Improvement
Phase the refactor by layer, highest-churn first. Per node: trim material owned
elsewhere, link instead of repeat, then split along a concern seam. Decision
records are tightened (never split). Remove each path from
`node_size_allowlist.txt` as its node comes under budget; when the list is empty,
delete the allow-list mechanism and enforce `--strict` with no exemptions.

## Risk And Blast Radius
Splitting changes paths, so every rename must update the decision log,
traceability map, and inbound links in the same change. Over-aggressive trimming
can drop rationale a decision record should own — move it, do not delete.
